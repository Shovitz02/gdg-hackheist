About

Learn more about our upcoming event and what to expect!

HackHeist 2026 is an exciting opportunity for tech enthusiasts, aspiring developers, and creative minds to come together, exchange ideas, and explore innovative solutions to real-world challenges. Organized by GDG on Campus MIET, the event celebrates creativity, collaboration, and the power of technology.

Whether you're passionate about coding, problem-solving, or exploring new technologies, HackHeist is a chance to challenge yourself, learn something new, and turn your ideas into something meaningful.

Think. Build. Innovate. Make your mark at HackHeist 2026.

Hackathon

Put your skills to the test in an exciting coding challenge! Collaborate with creative minds, solve real-world problems, and turn innovative ideas into working solutions. Build, experiment, and compete to bring your vision to life at HackHeist 2026.

Workshops

Learn by doing in interactive, hands-on workshops led by passionate tech enthusiasts. Explore new tools, develop practical skills, and gain valuable experience that you can use to bring your ideas to life.

Networking
